Case studies

Four projects, told plainly

Challenge, action, result, and what I would change.

People Operations built from zero

Ridecell | Dec 2018 - Jan 2026

Challenge

Ridecell had no documentation beyond outdated files, headcount lived in spreadsheets, and employees had no tracked channel for questions.

What I did

Built a 20+ procedure SOP library with owners, launched ASKPeople (Jira and Confluence, tiered SLAs), and made Sapling the HR single source of truth, integrated with ADP and Zapier connections.

Result

Scaled from 3 countries to 5 with 6 consecutive clean SOC 2 Type 2 audits, approval cycles cut from 1-2 days to 2-3 hours, and 5-10 tickets a day resolved in 24-48 hours.

What I would do differently

I'd have started the SOP library and ticketing system in the first months. Documenting as I went would have been easier than catching up later.

A benefits renewal with one month to spare

Ridecell | Dec 2018 - Jan 2026

One approval path for every HRIS change

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

HRIS changes ran through an undocumented, unapproved process that created audit risk, and early pushback said a form would be too process-heavy.

What I did

Built a Claude-powered Employee Action Form requiring documentation and approval before any HRIS change, plus Notion New Hire and Offboarding workflows.

Result

100% adoption within one to two months, an audit trail that had not existed, and preboarding within 24 hours of offer acceptance.

What I would do differently

I'd have piloted the form with one team first. Some people said it was too process-heavy, and early proof from a small group might have won them over faster.

  • Claude
  • Rippling
  • Notion
  • Slack
  • Payroll and IT approvals

A reorganization with cost visibility

Prezent AI | Feb 2026 - Apr 2026

Challenge

A reorganization impacted 18 employees across the US and India, and leaders had no view of the cost.

What I did

Used AI-enabled severance and cost modeling (Claude and Gemini) that calculated severance, COBRA cost, and COBRA months owed and drafted agreements, tracked on a shared spreadsheet for Payroll and IT.

Result

First-time RIF cost visibility, and agreement prep cut from 20 minutes per employee to about 3 hours total.

What I would do differently

I'd have built the cost model before the reorganization was decided. Leaders could then have compared scenarios rather than just seeing the cost of the chosen one.

  • Claude
  • Gemini
  • COBRA
  • Shared spreadsheet
